Refund Policy

Last Updated: July 2026

At Fieldflow AI, we want you to be completely satisfied with your purchase. If you are not satisfied with our services, this Refund Policy outlines your rights and how you can request a refund.

1. Subscription Plans

Our monthly and annual subscription plans come with a 14-day money-back guarantee. If you are not completely satisfied with our service within the first 14 days of your initial purchase, you are eligible for a full refund, no questions asked.

2. Renewals

Subscription renewals (whether monthly or annual) are generally non-refundable. However, if you forget to cancel your subscription and contact us within 48 hours of the renewal charge, we will evaluate your refund request on a case-by-case basis.

3. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, please contact us at support@fieldflow.ai using the email address associated with your Fieldflow AI account. Please include your order details and the reason for your refund request.

Once your request is received and approved, your refund will be processed and a credit will automatically be applied to your original method of payment within 5-10 business days.
