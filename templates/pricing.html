Frequently Asked Questions

What happens during the 14-day free trial? ▼
You get full access to the Pro plan features for 14 days, with a limit of 100 WhatsApp reports. No credit card is required to start. At the end of the trial, you can choose a plan or your account will be paused (no data is deleted).
How does the WhatsApp integration work? ▼
Your field workers send voice notes, text messages, or photos to your dedicated WhatsApp number. Our AI instantly transcribes, translates (if needed), and structures the data into professional reports — no app download required.
What languages are supported? ▼
Field workers can send reports in any language — Spanish, Portuguese, Turkish, Arabic, and more. Our AI automatically translates everything into professional US construction English for your ERP system.
Can I cancel anytime? ▼
Yes, absolutely. There are no long-term contracts. You can cancel your subscription at any time, and you'll retain access until the end of your current billing period.
Do I need a Procore account? ▼
No! Procore integration is available on the Pro plan and above, but it's optional. The Starter plan works perfectly without any ERP — you can manage reports in the dashboard and export as CSV.
